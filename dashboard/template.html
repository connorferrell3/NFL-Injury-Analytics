Repeat offenders

Players drafted in the top 48 in more than one season, ranked by how many of those seasons they missed time. Across the NFL, getting hurt one season raises the next season's risk; among fantasy picks the effect is too small to confirm (p = 0.43).

By team

Average fantasy weeks lost per top-48 pick, by the player's Week 1 team. Most teams have only 10–15 picks across six seasons, and a permutation test finds these gaps are consistent with random chance (p = 0.17). Read this as a record of what happened, not a prediction.

How this was built

Snap-share starters

An offensive or defensive player who averaged at least 65% of his unit's snaps in the games he played (50% for running backs), minimum two games. Last season's starters also count if hurt early. About 22 per team.

Pedigree players

Any of: a first-round pick still on his rookie contract (up to year 5, ending early with an extension or new deal); a cap hit in the top 30% of his position group that season, from OverTheCap; or a Pro Bowl or All-Pro selection the season before. Prior-season honors are used because players who get hurt are less likely to be selected that same year.

Absences

A run of consecutive team games a player missed, counted only when there is injury evidence: an injury-related listing on the official report, or an Injured Reserve designation. Benchings, suspensions and personal absences are excluded. Releases and trades end the run.

Body region

Free-text report labels are mapped to 16 regions. Players sent straight to IR often never appear on the report while out, so the label comes from the week before or the practice report after they return. About 5% of absences, and a third of season-ending ones, still have no body part.

Repeat injuries

Next-season risk compares starters who were key players in back-to-back seasons. Same-region recurrence looks at each player's consecutive absences with a known body part; the chance rate is what you'd get if the next injury were drawn at random from the overall injury mix.

Fantasy

ADP from Fantasy Football Calculator's PPR 12-team drafts in the week before each season (2020–2025) and Sleeper's PPR ADP for the current season, since Fantasy Football Calculator only serves in-season drafts once the season starts. Weeks are counted from Week 1 even if the player never suited up.

Caveats

Current-season numbers cover only the weeks played so far and are kept out of the six-season averages. 2020 had a 16-game season and a separate COVID list, which is not counted as injury. Week 18 has extra one-game absences from playoff teams resting starters. Durations stop at the end of the regular season.

Data: nflverse injury reports, snap counts, weekly rosters, draft picks and OverTheCap contracts; Fantasy Football Calculator and Sleeper PPR ADP; Wikipedia Pro Bowl and All-Pro rosters. 2020 through the current regular season.
